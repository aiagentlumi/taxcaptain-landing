What We're Building

We're developing tools for tax professionals. Here's what we're working on — your feedback will shape what we ship.

📄

Document Reading

We're exploring how AI can read and extract data from W-2s, 1099s, receipts, and other common tax documents.

🎯

Deduction Checklist Tools

Tools to help your team organize and review potential deductions and credits — a second set of eyes, not a replacement for professional judgment.

👥

Client Portal

A place for clients to securely upload documents and check on their return status, so your team spends less time on back-and-forth emails.

🗺️

Multi-State Awareness

We're building with multi-state complexity in mind, so firms with clients across jurisdictions have better tooling.

🔍

Review Checklists

Checklists and validation prompts to support your team's existing quality control process before filing.

📊

Workflow Dashboard

A dashboard to see return progress, team workload, and client status in one view during tax season.
